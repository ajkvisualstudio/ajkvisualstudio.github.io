Founder & CEO
Meet the founder

Akash JohnKennedy.

The eye behind every AJK frame — six years in Dubai turning brands into stories people stop for.

Akash is an AI filmmaker, videographer and certified marketing professional who has worked with the Malayalam and Tamil film industry. He shoots, edits, grades and adds VFX, builds 3D in Blender and directs AI films — then puts the work in front of the right audience with social media and Meta ads.

He founded AJK Visual Studio on one simple rule: story first, craft second, and the details nobody notices until they're missing. Today the studio works with brands across real estate, retail and luxury, and with the fan communities who fill cinemas across the UAE.
